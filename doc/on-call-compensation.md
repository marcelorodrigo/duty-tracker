# On-Call (Standby) Compensation

You are paid for every hour you were on-call. The rate and maximum claimable hours depend on the day type.

## Rules

| Day type | Rate | Max hours |
|---|---|---|
| Mon–Sat, regular working day | Mon–Sat rate | 15 hours |
| Mon–Sat, non-working day | Mon–Sat rate | 24 hours |
| Sunday or public holiday | Sunday/Holiday rate (higher) | 24 hours |

### Key details

- A **day off** on a normally-working day is still treated as a regular working day — the 15-hour cap applies.
- **Part-time** schedules are respected: if you don't normally work on a given weekday, it counts as a non-working day.
- **Public holidays** falling on Mon–Sat use the Sunday/Holiday rate and are uncapped at 24 hours.

## MyHR entry

- Plan: `NL Allowances - Standby allowance`
- Option: select the appropriate rate (`Monday-Saturday` or `Sunday/Holiday`)
- Enter the date and hours for each day

## Shift boundary (Monday handover)

On-call shifts typically start and end on **Mondays at 14:00**. For convenience, you can register a single Monday with the full hours (capped at 15 for working days) instead of splitting across two Mondays.

However, if either Monday is a **holiday**, you must split them because different rates apply:

| Scenario | First Monday | Second Monday |
|---|---|---|
| Neither Monday is a holiday | 15h at Mon–Sat (single entry) | — |
| First Monday is a holiday | 10h at Sunday/Holiday (14:00–00:00) | 8h at Mon–Sat (00:00–08:00) |
| Second Monday is a holiday | 7h at Mon–Sat (17:00–00:00) | 14h at Sunday/Holiday (00:00–14:00) |

## Examples

### Single-day examples

| Scenario | Log |
|---|---|
| Monday (working day), on-call 6h outside business hours | 6h at Mon–Sat rate |
| Monday (working day), on-call all day and night | 15h at Mon–Sat rate (capped) |
| Saturday (non-working day), on-call all day | 24h at Mon–Sat rate |
| Saturday (working day for you), on-call all day | 15h at Mon–Sat rate |
| Sunday, on-call all day | 24h at Sunday/Holiday rate |
| Monday, public holiday, on-call all day | 24h at Sunday/Holiday rate |
| Monday (working day), took the day off, on-call all day | 15h at Mon–Sat rate; discuss time-for-time with manager |
| Monday, part-time (don't normally work Mon), on-call all day | 15h at Mon–Sat rate; discuss time-for-time with manager |
| Taking over a colleague's shift for 2 hours | 2h at applicable rate |
